Margin PDF privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026. This policy covers the Margin PDF Chrome extension.

Your documents

Margin PDF renders PDFs locally on your device. It receives PDF data that Chrome has already loaded, or reads a local file you select. It does not upload PDF contents, filenames, URLs, passwords, or reading activity to the developer or any third party.

Document bytes and passwords are held in memory while needed to open and display the document. Download saves the original document through Chrome. Printing uses Chrome’s print dialog and any printer or destination you choose.

Settings and optional reading positions

Appearance preferences, tab-title preferences, user-specified title domains and the reading-position preference are saved in local extension storage. Reading-position history is off by default. If you enable it, the extension saves page, scroll, zoom, rotation, and split-pane positions for up to 100 documents on this device. Documents are identified by a SHA-256 fingerprint of their contents. The saved records contain no document contents, filenames, or URLs. These fingerprints are identifiers, not a claim of anonymisation.

You can clear saved positions in Reader settings. Turning off “Remember reading positions” also deletes them. Removing the extension deletes its local storage. The extension does not use Chrome Sync.

Network access and links

The extension includes its reader code, PDF worker, fonts, and sample document. It has no analytics, advertising, tracking, accounts, or remote code. It does not request permission to read browsing history. Following an external link opens that destination in a new Chrome tab; the destination’s own privacy policy then applies.

Permissions

The storage permission saves preferences and optional reading positions. Chrome’s PDF-handler registration routes PDFs to the extension. The scripting permission changes a full-page PDF's browser tab title when you enable that option. Enabling PDF titles requests optional access to your selected domains, or to all HTTP/HTTPS websites if you choose that scope. This feature is off by default, and the domain list starts with arxiv.org. The extension does not inject PDF code into websites or rename pages containing embedded PDFs. Browsing-history, downloads and native-messaging permissions are not requested.

Questions

Use the developer contact listed on Margin PDF’s Chrome Web Store listing for privacy questions.
